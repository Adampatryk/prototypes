Three ways to book.

Every corporate booking is quoted per therapist, per session, on hours and headcount.

  • One-off day

    Wellbeing days and events. Staff appreciation, awareness weeks, conferences, Christmas. Any headcount, we scale the team.

  • Recurring

    Monthly or fortnightly programmes. A familiar therapist on a set day, a sign-up sheet for staff, one simple invoice.

  • Large scale

    Multi-therapist events. Big offices, universities and multi-site wellbeing weeks, covered by a team of twenty-plus. The Hakim Group annual conference at Telford had more than 2,000 people attending.

Desk or chair?

  • Desk massage

    Short, at the employee's own desk. Nothing to set up, nobody leaves their seat.

  • Chair massage

    We bring the massage chair. Needs a small, ideally secluded space, roughly 2m by 2m, with a plug socket.

Questions office managers ask.

How many people can you see in a day?

One therapist typically sees 18 to 24 people on fifteen to twenty minute slots. For bigger teams we add therapists so everyone is seen in one day.

Do staff need to undress or use oils?

No. Seated chair massage is done fully clothed with no oils, so people can go straight back to work.

What do you need from us?

A quiet space of roughly 2m by 2m per therapist and a plug socket. We bring the chairs, towels, music and sign-up sheet.

Are you insured?

Yes. All therapists are qualified and fully insured.

Which areas do you cover?

Leicester, Lincoln, Grantham, Boston, Nottingham, Mansfield, Kettering, Melton Mowbray, Derby, Chesterfield, Worksop, Hinckley, Northampton, Loughborough and Corby.
