How the day runs.

A typical single-therapist day. Bigger teams get more therapists, so everyone is seen on the same day.

Three ways to book.

Every corporate booking is quoted per therapist, per session, on hours and headcount.

  • One-off day

    Wellbeing days and events. Staff appreciation, awareness weeks, conferences, Christmas. Any headcount, we scale the team.

  • Recurring

    Monthly or fortnightly programmes. A familiar therapist on a set day.

  • Large scale

    Multi-therapist events. Big offices, universities and multi-site wellbeing weeks, covered by a team of twenty-plus.

Please note

Pamper party prices do not apply to corporate massage, wellbeing days or ongoing projects. All corporate enquiries are quoted individually.

Desk or chair?

  • Desk massage

    Short, at the employee's own desk. Nothing to set up, nobody leaves their seat.

  • Chair massage

    We bring the massage chair. Needs a small, ideally secluded space.
