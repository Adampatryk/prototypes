Which areas do you cover?

Nottingham, Derby and Leicester, plus Loughborough, Lincoln, Grantham, Boston, Mansfield, Kettering, Melton Mowbray, Chesterfield, Worksop, Hinckley, Northampton and Corby. Larger corporate bookings further afield are welcome.

What do you need from us?

A quiet space, a plug socket and roughly 2m × 2m per therapist. We bring everything else.

Are you insured?

Yes, every therapist is qualified and fully insured.

How is corporate work priced?

Per therapist per session, based on hours and headcount; regular programmes are invoiced monthly. Party per-person rates don't apply.
