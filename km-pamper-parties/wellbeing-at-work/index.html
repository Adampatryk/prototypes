How a wellbeing day runs

We arrive, set up, and your team just turns up

  • 08:45
    We arrive and set upMassage chair, music, towels and a sign-up sheet, in a meeting room or quiet corner. All we need is a plug socket and about 2m × 2m per therapist.
  • 09:00
    First slot15-minute seated massages, fully clothed, no oils. Straight back to the desk.
  • 16:30
    Last slot and pack downAround 18 to 24 people seen per therapist. Bigger teams get more therapists, so everyone is seen on the same day.
  • Next
    Repeat monthly, if you likeMost clients move to a regular programme with one simple monthly invoice.

Every corporate booking is quoted per therapist, per session, based on hours and headcount. Pamper party per-person rates don't apply. Itemised quotes go out the same working day.

FAQs

Questions HR and office managers ask

How many people can you see in a day?

One therapist typically sees 18 to 24 people on 15-minute slots. For bigger teams we add therapists so everyone is seen in one day.

Do staff need to undress or use oils?

No. Seated chair massage is done fully clothed with no oils, so people can go straight back to work.

Are you insured, and can you supply certificates?

Yes. All therapists are qualified and fully insured, and we can provide insurance and qualification documents for procurement.

Which areas do you cover?

Nottingham, Derby, Leicester and the wider East Midlands. Larger bookings further afield are welcome.
