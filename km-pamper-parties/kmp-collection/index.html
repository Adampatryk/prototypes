How a day runs

  • Set-up

    A meeting room or quiet corner, roughly 2m by 2m and a plug socket per therapist. We bring the chair, towels, music and a sign-up sheet.

  • Each session

    Fifteen to twenty minutes, seated and fully clothed, with no oils. Straight back to work afterwards.

  • In a day

    Around 18 to 24 people per therapist. Bigger teams get more therapists.

  • Desk or chair

    Desk massage is short and happens at the employee's own desk. Chair massage uses our massage chair and needs a small, ideally secluded space.

A Relaxing Hen Party Pamper Experience at Red Roofs Barn

October 2026

A hen party is often a busy weekend of celebrations, so adding some time to relax and unwind can be a lovely way for everyone to enjoy a little time out together.

KM Pamper Parties recently provided a mobile pamper experience for a group of six celebrating a hen party at Red Roofs Barn.

We brought everything needed to create a comfortable treatment area within the accommodation, allowing the group to enjoy their treatments without having to travel anywhere or interrupt their weekend plans.

One of the benefits of our mobile pamper parties is that we set up within your chosen accommodation. Whether you’re staying in a holiday cottage, group house or other private accommodation, we bring our professional treatment equipment and products with us.

Treatments are organised into a schedule before the booking so everyone knows their allocated treatment time. Once we know the number of guests, their chosen treatments and the time available, we put together a treatment schedule to help everything run smoothly.

The group gave lovely positive feedback about their treatments, which was wonderful to hear.

KM Pamper Parties provides mobile massage, beauty and holistic treatments for hen parties, birthdays, girls’ weekends, group getaways and other special occasions.

With more than 22 years of experience, our aim is to provide a professional, friendly and well-organised service while giving guests the opportunity to relax and enjoy quality treatments in the comfort of their chosen accommodation.

If you’re planning a hen party or group stay, simply get in touch with your date, location and approximate group size and we can help you plan your pamper experience.

Enhancing Employee Well-being: Onsite Chair Massages at RAF Lincolnshire Training Day

March 2025

The Royal Air Force (RAF) Wellbeing Day in Lincolnshire marked a significant step towards integrating holistic wellness into military training programs. For the first time, the defence team experienced onsite chair massages during their training day event, aiming to enhance both physical and mental well-being.

Pioneering Onsite Chair Massage in Military Training

Introducing corporate chair massage services into the structured environment of military training was a novel initiative. Initially, some staff members were sceptical about receiving massages in the workplace. However, this approach aligns with broader trends in corporate wellness programs that have shown significant benefits in various professional settings. Studies indicate that chair massages can decrease anxiety, relieve muscle tension, and improve overall employee productivity.

Transformative Feedback from Defence Personnel

Post-event feedback revealed a positive shift in perception among the defence team. Many expressed gratitude for the opportunity to experience workplace massage services, noting substantial benefits such as reduced stress levels and alleviated muscle tension. This mirrors findings from corporate environments where onsite chair massages have led to improved employee morale and productivity.

The Strategic Advantage of Onsite Chair Massages

Incorporating onsite chair massages into the RAF’s training regimen offers several strategic advantages:

  • Stress Reduction: Regular massage sessions have been shown to lower stress and anxiety, contributing to better focus and decision-making.
  • Enhanced Physical Readiness: Addressing muscle tension and discomfort ensures personnel are physically prepared for the demands of their roles.
  • Boosted Morale and Cohesion: Offering wellness services demonstrates a commitment to personnel well-being, fostering a supportive and cohesive team environment.

Looking Ahead: Integrating Wellness into Military Culture

The success of the Wellbeing Day in Lincolnshire sets a precedent for future initiatives within the RAF and potentially other branches of the military. Embracing corporate wellness solutions like onsite chair massages can lead to a more resilient and effective defence force, ready to meet the challenges of their demanding roles.

Investing in the well-being of military personnel is not just a compassionate approach but a strategic imperative, ensuring that those who serve are at their best, both physically and mentally.

Hakim Group’s Annual Conference 2024: A Weekend of Well-being and Professional Growth

March 2025

The Hakim Group Annual Conference took place in December 2024 at the Telford International Centre, bringing together over 2,000 optical industry professionals for a weekend focused on success and well-being.

Well-being Interventions: Onsite Chair Massages and Makeup Sessions

A team of 22 therapists provided onsite chair massages and makeup sessions throughout the weekend. These services were in high demand, with all slots fully booked, reflecting the attendees’ appreciation for relaxation and self-care opportunities.

Positive Feedback and Anticipation for Future Events

Delegates expressed immense satisfaction with the well-being services, noting that the treatments left them feeling rejuvenated and more focused for the day’s sessions. The success of these interventions has heightened anticipation for the next conference, scheduled for November 2025.

About the Telford International Centre

The Telford International Centre is a premier event venue in the heart of the Midlands, offering over 15,000 square meters of versatile event space. Its modern facilities and central location make it an ideal choice for large-scale conferences and exhibitions.

Investing in attendee well-being not only enhances the conference experience but also fosters a more engaged and productive professional community.

KM Pamper Parties Enhances Employee Wellness at Microlise with Onsite Chair Massages in Eastwood, Nottingham

February 2025

KM Pamper Parties recently collaborated with Microlise to host a Corporate Wellbeing Day at their Nottingham branch in Eastwood, offering onsite chair massages that were fully booked and enthusiastically received by the staff.

Tailored Onsite Chair Massages

Employees had the opportunity to enjoy personalised chair massages, with options to incorporate hot stones over clothing. This approach provided a warming, relaxing experience that effectively eased tight muscles. The convenience of receiving these treatments onsite allowed staff to seamlessly integrate relaxation into their workday.

Aromatherapy Enhancements

To further enhance the experience, aromatherapy oils were available, offering a delightful way for employees to take a moment of calm amidst their busy schedules. The soothing scents contributed to a tranquil atmosphere, promoting mental clarity and stress relief.

Positive Staff Feedback

Feedback from Microlise employees was overwhelmingly positive. Participants reported feeling more relaxed and appreciated the opportunity to unwind without leaving the workplace. Such initiatives not only boost morale but also demonstrate a company’s commitment to the well-being of its team.
